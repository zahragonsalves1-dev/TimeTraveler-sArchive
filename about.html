About

Biography & background

A brief account of who I am, what I study, and my future career goals.

I am an undergraduate history student at Temple University where my coursework centers on Islamic history and historical preservation. My interest in history has pointed me toward the institutions that preserve it: museums, archives, and libraries.

My long term goal is to become a research librarian working at the intersection of historical scholarship, archival stewardship, and public access to knowledge. I am especially drawn to understanding the role that universities, museums, archives and libraries play in making information more accessible.

I also hold a longstanding interest in Islamic history and the preservation of Islamic cultural heritage. Over time I hope to develop real expertise in this area and to eventually build an institution devoted to it.
