Projects

Research, digital humanities, archival, museum, and community work — documented as it happens, not after the fact.

How to use this page: replace the placeholder projects in /js/data.js (the PROJECTS array) with your own. Each project can link out to its own page, a PDF, images, or an external resource via its href. To add a new project later, copy an existing object in the array and edit the fields — title, role, date, description, what you learned, and skills.

A note on project pages

Giving each project its own page

As your projects grow more substantial, you may want a dedicated page for each one — with a fuller write-up, images, and documentation. Duplicate about.html as a starting template, adjust the content, and link to it from that project's href in data.js.
