Hello! My name is Zahra Gonsalves. I am currently a student at Temple University majoring in History. I am building a career in library and information science with a concentration on archival and museum studies, special collections, and historical preservation. I am a champion for the accessibility of knowledge and I believe that understanding our past is imperative to building our future. On this site you will find a collection of my writings, research and projects.

About this site

This site takes its name from the idea that archives, museums, and libraries have the power to take us through time and allow us to discover the hidden knowledge that our history holds. The Time Traveler's Archive is where I gather my coursework, writing and research as I work towards a career preserving and sharing the past.
